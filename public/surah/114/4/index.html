سورة الناس، الآية ٤Surah An-Nas, ayah 4 (114:4)

مِن شَرِّ ٱلۡوَسۡوَاسِ ٱلۡخَنَّاسِ ٤

الصفحة ٦٠٤ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 604 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

"From the evil of the whisperer (devil who whispers evil in the hearts of men) who withdraws (from his whispering in one’s heart after one remembers Allâh).

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex

التفسير الميسر
