سورة القيامة، الآية ١٥Surah Al-Qiyamah, ayah 15 (75:15)

وَلَوۡ أَلۡقَىٰ مَعَاذِيرَهُۥ ١٥

الصفحة ٥٧٧ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 577 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

Though he may put forth his excuses (to cover his evil deeds).

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
