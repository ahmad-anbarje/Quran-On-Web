سورة الإسراء، الآية ٥١Surah Al-Isra, ayah 51 (17:51)

أَوۡ خَلۡقًا مِّمَّا يَكۡبُرُ فِى صُدُورِكُمۡ فَسَيَقُولُونَ مَن يُعِيدُنَا قُلِ ٱلَّذِى فَطَرَكُمۡ أَوَّلَ مَرَّةٍ فَسَيُنۡغِضُونَ إِلَيۡكَ رُءُوسَهُمۡ وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هُوَ قُلۡ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَكُونَ قَرِيبًا ٥١

The meaning in English

"Or some created thing that is yet greater (or harder) in your breasts (thoughts to be resurrected, even then you shall be resurrected)." Then, they will say: "Who shall bring us back (to life)?" Say: "He Who created you first!" Then, they will shake their heads at you and say: "When will that be?" Say: "Perhaps it is near!"

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
