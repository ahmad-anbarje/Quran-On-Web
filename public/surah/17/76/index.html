سورة الإسراء، الآية ٧٦Surah Al-Isra, ayah 76 (17:76)

وَإِن كَادُواْ لَيَسۡتَفِزُّونَكَ مِنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ لِيُخۡرِجُوكَ مِنۡهَا وَإِذًا لَّا يَلۡبَثُونَ خِلَٰفَكَ إِلَّا قَلِيلًا ٧٦

الصفحة ٢٩٠ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 290 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

And verily they were about to frighten you so much as to drive you out from the land. But in that case they would not have stayed (therein) after you, except for a little while.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
