سورة الفرقان، الآية ٣٣Surah Al-Furqan, ayah 33 (25:33)

وَلَا يَأۡتُونَكَ بِمَثَلٍ إِلَّا جِئۡنَٰكَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَأَحۡسَنَ تَفۡسِيرًا ٣٣

الصفحة ٣٦٣ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 363 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

And no example or similitude do they bring (to oppose or to find fault in you or in this Qur’ân), but We reveal to you the truth (against that similitude or example), and the better explanation thereof.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
