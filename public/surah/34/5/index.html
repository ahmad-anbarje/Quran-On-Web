سورة سبإ، الآية ٥Surah Saba, ayah 5 (34:5)

وَٱلَّذِينَ سَعَوۡ فِىٓ ءَايَٰتِنَا مُعَٰجِزِينَ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ مِّن رِّجۡزٍ أَلِيمٌ ٥

الصفحة ٤٢٨ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 428 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

But those who strive against Our Ayât (proofs, evidence, verses, lessons, signs, revelations, etc.) to frustrate them - those, for them will be a severe painful torment.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
