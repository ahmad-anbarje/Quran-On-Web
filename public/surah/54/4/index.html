سورة القمر، الآية ٤Surah Al-Qamar, ayah 4 (54:4)

وَلَقَدۡ جَآءَهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَنۢبَآءِ مَا فِيهِ مُزۡدَجَرٌ ٤

الصفحة ٥٢٨ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 528 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

And indeed there has come to them news (in this Qur’ân) wherein there is (enough warning) to check (them from evil),

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
