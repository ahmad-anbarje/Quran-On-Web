سورة القمر، الآية ٣١Surah Al-Qamar, ayah 31 (54:31)

إِنَّآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ صَيۡحَةً وَٰحِدَةً فَكَانُواْ كَهَشِيمِ ٱلۡمُحۡتَظِرِ ٣١

الصفحة ٥٣٠ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 530 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

Verily, We sent against them a single Saihah (torment - awful cry), and they became like the stubble of a fold-builder.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
