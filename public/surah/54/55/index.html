سورة القمر، الآية ٥٥Surah Al-Qamar, ayah 55 (54:55)

فِى مَقۡعَدِ صِدۡقٍ عِندَ مَلِيكٍ مُّقۡتَدِرٍۭ ٥٥

الصفحة ٥٣١ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 531 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

In a seat of truth (i.e. Paradise), near the Omnipotent King (Allâh, the One, the All-Blessed, the Most High, the Owner of Majesty and Honour).

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
