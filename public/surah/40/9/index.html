سورة غافر، الآية ٩Surah Ghafir, ayah 9 (40:9)

وَقِهِمُ ٱلسَّيِّـَٔاتِ وَمَن تَقِ ٱلسَّيِّـَٔاتِ يَوۡمَئِذٍ فَقَدۡ رَحِمۡتَهُۥ وَذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ ٩

الصفحة ٤٦٨ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 468 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

"And save them from (the punishment, for what they did of) the sins, and whomsoever You save from (the punishment for what he did of) the sins (i.e. pardon him) that Day, him verily, You have taken into mercy." And that is the supreme success.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
