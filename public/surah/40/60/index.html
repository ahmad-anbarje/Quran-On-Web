سورة غافر، الآية ٦٠Surah Ghafir, ayah 60 (40:60)

وَقَالَ رَبُّكُمُ ٱدۡعُونِىٓ أَسۡتَجِبۡ لَكُمۡ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ عَنۡ عِبَادَتِى سَيَدۡخُلُونَ جَهَنَّمَ دَاخِرِينَ ٦٠

الصفحة ٤٧٤ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة

The meaning in English

And your Lord said: "Invoke Me, [i.e. believe in My Oneness (Islâmic Monotheism) and ask Me for anything] I will respond to your (invocation). Verily! Those who scorn My worship [i.e. do not invoke Me, and do not believe in My Oneness, (Islâmic Monotheism)] they will surely enter Hell in humiliation!"

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
