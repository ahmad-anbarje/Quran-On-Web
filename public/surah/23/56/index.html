سورة المؤمنون، الآية ٥٦Surah Al-Mu'minun, ayah 56 (23:56)

نُسَارِعُ لَهُمۡ فِى ٱلۡخَيۡرَٰتِ بَل لَّا يَشۡعُرُونَ ٥٦

الصفحة ٣٤٥ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 345 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

We hasten unto them with good things. Nay, [it is a Fitnah (trial) in this worldly life so that they will have no share of good things in the Hereafter] but they perceive not. [Tafsir Al-Qurtubi].

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
