سورة المؤمنون، الآية ٤٩Surah Al-Mu'minun, ayah 49 (23:49)

وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا مُوسَى ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَهۡتَدُونَ ٤٩

The meaning in English

And indeed We gave Mûsâ (Moses) the Scripture, that they may be guided.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
