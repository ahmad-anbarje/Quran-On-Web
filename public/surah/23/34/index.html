سورة المؤمنون، الآية ٣٤Surah Al-Mu'minun, ayah 34 (23:34)

وَلَئِنۡ أَطَعۡتُم بَشَرًا مِّثۡلَكُمۡ إِنَّكُمۡ إِذًا لَّخَٰسِرُونَ ٣٤

الصفحة ٣٤٤ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 344 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

"If you were to obey a human being like yourselves, then verily you indeed would be losers.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
