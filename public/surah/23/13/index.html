سورة المؤمنون، الآية ١٣Surah Al-Mu'minun, ayah 13 (23:13)

ثُمَّ جَعَلۡنَٰهُ نُطۡفَةً فِى قَرَارٍ مَّكِينٍ ١٣

الصفحة ٣٤٢ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 342 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

Thereafter We made him (the offspring of Adam) as a Nutfah (mixed drops of the male and female sexual discharge and lodged it) in a safe lodging (womb of the woman).

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
