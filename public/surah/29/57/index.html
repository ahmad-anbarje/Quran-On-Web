سورة العنكبوت، الآية ٥٧Surah Al-'Ankabut, ayah 57 (29:57)

كُلُّ نَفۡسٍ ذَآئِقَةُ ٱلۡمَوۡتِ ثُمَّ إِلَيۡنَا تُرۡجَعُونَ ٥٧

The meaning in English

Everyone shall taste death. Then unto Us you shall be returned.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex

التفسير الميسر
