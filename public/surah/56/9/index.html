سورة الواقعة، الآية ٩Surah Al-Waqi'ah, ayah 9 (56:9)

وَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَشۡـَٔمَةِ مَآ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَشۡـَٔمَةِ ٩

الصفحة ٥٣٤ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 534 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

And those on the Left Hand (i.e. those who will be given their Record in their left hands) - how (unfortunate) will be those on the Left Hand! (As a disgrace for them, because they will enter Hell).

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
