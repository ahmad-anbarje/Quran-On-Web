سورة الهمزة، الآية ٩Surah Al-Humazah, ayah 9 (104:9)

فِى عَمَدٍ مُّمَدَّدَةٍۭ ٩

The meaning in English

In pillars stretched forth (i.e. they will be punished in the Fire with pillars).

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
