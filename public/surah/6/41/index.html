سورة الأنعام، الآية ٤١Surah Al-An'am, ayah 41 (6:41)

بَلۡ إِيَّاهُ تَدۡعُونَ فَيَكۡشِفُ مَا تَدۡعُونَ إِلَيۡهِ إِن شَآءَ وَتَنسَوۡنَ مَا تُشۡرِكُونَ ٤١

الصفحة ١٣٢ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 132 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

Nay! To Him Alone you would call, and, if He wills, He would remove that (distress) for which you call upon Him, and you would forget at that time whatever partners you joined (with Him in worship)!

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
