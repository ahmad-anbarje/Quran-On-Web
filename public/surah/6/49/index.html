سورة الأنعام، الآية ٤٩Surah Al-An'am, ayah 49 (6:49)

وَٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا يَمَسُّهُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابُ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَفۡسُقُونَ ٤٩

الصفحة ١٣٣ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 133 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

But those who reject Our Ayât (proofs, evidence, verses, lessons, signs, revelations, etc.), the torment will touch them for their disbelief (and for their belying the Message of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم). [Tafsir Al-Qurtubî].

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
