سورة الأنعام، الآية ٩٧Surah Al-An'am, ayah 97 (6:97)

وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِى جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلنُّجُومَ لِتَهۡتَدُواْ بِهَا فِى ظُلُمَٰتِ ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِ قَدۡ فَصَّلۡنَا ٱلۡـَٔايَٰتِ لِقَوۡمٍ يَعۡلَمُونَ ٩٧

الصفحة ١٤٠ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 140 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

It is He Who has set the stars for you, so that you may guide your course with their help through the darkness of the land and the sea. We have (indeed) explained in detail Our Ayât (proofs, evidence, verses, lessons, signs, Revelations, etc.) for people who know.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
