سورة الأنعام، الآية ١٣١Surah Al-An'am, ayah 131 (6:131)

ذَٰلِكَ أَن لَّمۡ يَكُن رَّبُّكَ مُهۡلِكَ ٱلۡقُرَىٰ بِظُلۡمٍ وَأَهۡلُهَا غَٰفِلُونَ ١٣١

الصفحة ١٤٥ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 145 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

This is because your Lord would not destroy the (populations of) towns for their wrong-doing (i.e. associating others in worship along with Allâh) while their people were unaware (so the Messengers were sent).

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
