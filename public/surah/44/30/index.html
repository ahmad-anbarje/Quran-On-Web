سورة الدخان، الآية ٣٠Surah Ad-Dukhan, ayah 30 (44:30)

وَلَقَدۡ نَجَّيۡنَا بَنِىٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ مِنَ ٱلۡعَذَابِ ٱلۡمُهِينِ ٣٠

The meaning in English

And indeed We saved the Children of Israel from the humiliating torment:

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex

التفسير الميسر
