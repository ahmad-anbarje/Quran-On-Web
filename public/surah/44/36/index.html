سورة الدخان، الآية ٣٦Surah Ad-Dukhan, ayah 36 (44:36)

فَأۡتُواْ بِـَٔابَآئِنَآ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ ٣٦

The meaning in English

"Then bring back our forefathers, if you speak the truth!"

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex

التفسير الميسر
