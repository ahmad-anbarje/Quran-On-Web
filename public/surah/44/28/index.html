سورة الدخان، الآية ٢٨Surah Ad-Dukhan, ayah 28 (44:28)

كَذَٰلِكَ وَأَوۡرَثۡنَٰهَا قَوۡمًا ءَاخَرِينَ ٢٨

الصفحة ٤٩٧ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 497 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

Thus (it was)! And We made other people inherit them (i.e. We made the Children of Israel to inherit the kingdom of Egypt).

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex

التفسير الميسر
