سورة الدخان، الآية ٤٠Surah Ad-Dukhan, ayah 40 (44:40)

إِنَّ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡفَصۡلِ مِيقَٰتُهُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ ٤٠

الصفحة ٤٩٨ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 498 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

Verily, the Day of Judgement (when Allâh will judge between the creatures) is the time appointed for all of them -

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
