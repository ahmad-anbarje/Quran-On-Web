سورة الدخان، الآية ٢٢Surah Ad-Dukhan, ayah 22 (44:22)

فَدَعَا رَبَّهُۥٓ أَنَّ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ قَوۡمٌ مُّجۡرِمُونَ ٢٢

الصفحة ٤٩٧ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 497 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

(But they were aggressive), so he [Mûsâ (Moses)] called upon his Lord (saying): "These are indeed the people who are Mujrimûn (disbelievers, polytheists, sinners, criminals)."

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
