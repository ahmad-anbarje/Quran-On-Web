سورة الفلق، الآية ٤Surah Al-Falaq, ayah 4 (113:4)

وَمِن شَرِّ ٱلنَّفَّٰثَٰتِ فِى ٱلۡعُقَدِ ٤

The meaning in English

"And from the evil of those who practise witchcraft when they blow in the knots,

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
