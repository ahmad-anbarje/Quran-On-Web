سورة الرحمن، الآية ٢٢Surah Ar-Rahman, ayah 22 (55:22)

يَخۡرُجُ مِنۡهُمَا ٱللُّؤۡلُؤُ وَٱلۡمَرۡجَانُ ٢٢

The meaning in English

Out of them both come out pearl and coral.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
