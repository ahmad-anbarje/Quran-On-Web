مُتَّكِـِٔينَ عَلَىٰ رَفۡرَفٍ خُضۡرٍ وَعَبۡقَرِىٍّ حِسَانٍ ٧٦

The meaning in English

Reclining on green cushions and rich beautiful mattresses.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex

التفسير الميسر
