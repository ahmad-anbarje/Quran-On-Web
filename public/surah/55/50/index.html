سورة الرحمن، الآية ٥٠Surah Ar-Rahman, ayah 50 (55:50)

فِيهِمَا عَيۡنَانِ تَجۡرِيَانِ ٥٠

The meaning in English

In them (both) will be two springs flowing (free).

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
