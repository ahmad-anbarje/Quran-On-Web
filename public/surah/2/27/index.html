سورة البقرة، الآية ٢٧Surah Al-Baqarah, ayah 27 (2:27)

ٱلَّذِينَ يَنقُضُونَ عَهۡدَ ٱللَّهِ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مِيثَٰقِهِۦ وَيَقۡطَعُونَ مَآ أَمَرَ ٱللَّهُ بِهِۦٓ أَن يُوصَلَ وَيُفۡسِدُونَ فِى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡخَٰسِرُونَ ٢٧

الصفحة ٥ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 5 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

Those who break Allâh’s Covenant after ratifying it, and sever what Allâh has ordered to be joined (as regards Allâh’s religion of Islâmic Monotheism, and to practise its laws on the earth and also as regards keeping good relations with kith and kin), and do mischief on earth, it is they who are the losers.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
