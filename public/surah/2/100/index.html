سورة البقرة، الآية ١٠٠Surah Al-Baqarah, ayah 100 (2:100)

أَوَكُلَّمَا عَٰهَدُواْ عَهۡدًا نَّبَذَهُۥ فَرِيقٌ مِّنۡهُم بَلۡ أَكۡثَرُهُمۡ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ ١٠٠

الصفحة ١٥ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 15 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

Is it not (the case) that every time they make a covenant, some party among them throw it aside? Nay! (the truth is:) most of them believe not.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
