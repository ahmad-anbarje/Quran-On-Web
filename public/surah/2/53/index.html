سورة البقرة، الآية ٥٣Surah Al-Baqarah, ayah 53 (2:53)

وَإِذۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا مُوسَى ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡفُرۡقَانَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَهۡتَدُونَ ٥٣

الصفحة ٨ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 8 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

And (remember) when We gave Mûsâ (Moses) the Scripture [the Taurât (Torah)] and the criterion (of right and wrong) so that you may be guided aright."

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
