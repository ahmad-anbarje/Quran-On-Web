سورة البقرة، الآية ٢٠٦Surah Al-Baqarah, ayah 206 (2:206)

وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُ ٱتَّقِ ٱللَّهَ أَخَذَتۡهُ ٱلۡعِزَّةُ بِٱلۡإِثۡمِ فَحَسۡبُهُۥ جَهَنَّمُ وَلَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمِهَادُ ٢٠٦

الصفحة ٣٢ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 32 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

And when it is said to him, "Fear Allâh", he is led by arrogance to (more) crime. So enough for him is Hell, and worst indeed is that place to rest!

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
