سورة آل عمران، الآية ١٦٨Surah Ali 'Imran, ayah 168 (3:168)

ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُواْ لِإِخۡوَٰنِهِمۡ وَقَعَدُواْ لَوۡ أَطَاعُونَا مَا قُتِلُواْ قُلۡ فَٱدۡرَءُواْ عَنۡ أَنفُسِكُمُ ٱلۡمَوۡتَ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ ١٦٨

الصفحة ٧٢ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 72 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

(They are) the ones who said about their killed brethren while they themselves sat (at home): "If only they had listened to us, they would not have been killed." Say: "Avert death from your ownselves, if you speak the truth."

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
