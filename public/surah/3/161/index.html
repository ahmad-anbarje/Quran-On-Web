سورة آل عمران، الآية ١٦١Surah Ali 'Imran, ayah 161 (3:161)

وَمَا كَانَ لِنَبِىٍّ أَن يَغُلَّ وَمَن يَغۡلُلۡ يَأۡتِ بِمَا غَلَّ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ ثُمَّ تُوَفَّىٰ كُلُّ نَفۡسٍ مَّا كَسَبَتۡ وَهُمۡ لَا يُظۡلَمُونَ ١٦١

الصفحة ٧١ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 71 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

It is not for any Prophet to take illegally a part of booty (Ghulul ), and whosoever deceives his companions as regards the booty, he shall bring forth on the Day of Resurrection that which he took (illegally). Then every person shall be paid in full what he has earned, and they shall not be dealt with unjustly.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
