سورة آل عمران، الآية ٤١Surah Ali 'Imran, ayah 41 (3:41)

قَالَ رَبِّ ٱجۡعَل لِّىٓ ءَايَةً قَالَ ءَايَتُكَ أَلَّا تُكَلِّمَ ٱلنَّاسَ ثَلَٰثَةَ أَيَّامٍ إِلَّا رَمۡزًا وَٱذۡكُر رَّبَّكَ كَثِيرًا وَسَبِّحۡ بِٱلۡعَشِىِّ وَٱلۡإِبۡكَٰرِ ٤١

الصفحة ٥٥ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 55 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

He said: "O my Lord! Make a sign for me." Allâh said: "Your sign is that you shall not speak to mankind for three days except with signals. And remember your Lord much (by praising Him again and again), and glorify (Him) in the afternoon and in the morning."

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
