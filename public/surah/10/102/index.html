سورة يونس، الآية ١٠٢Surah Yunus, ayah 102 (10:102)

فَهَلۡ يَنتَظِرُونَ إِلَّا مِثۡلَ أَيَّامِ ٱلَّذِينَ خَلَوۡاْ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ قُلۡ فَٱنتَظِرُوٓاْ إِنِّى مَعَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡمُنتَظِرِينَ ١٠٢

الصفحة ٢٢٠ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 220 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

Then do they wait for (anything) save for (a destruction) like that of the days of the men who passed away before them? Say: "Wait then, I am (too) with you among those who wait."

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
