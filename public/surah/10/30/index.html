سورة يونس، الآية ٣٠Surah Yunus, ayah 30 (10:30)

هُنَالِكَ تَبۡلُواْ كُلُّ نَفۡسٍ مَّآ أَسۡلَفَتۡ وَرُدُّوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ مَوۡلَىٰهُمُ ٱلۡحَقِّ وَضَلَّ عَنۡهُم مَّا كَانُواْ يَفۡتَرُونَ ٣٠

الصفحة ٢١٢ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 212 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

There! Every person will know (exactly) what he had earned before and they will be brought back to Allâh, their rightful Maulâ (Lord), and their invented false deities will vanish from them.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
