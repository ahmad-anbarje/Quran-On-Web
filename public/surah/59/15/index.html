سورة الحشر، الآية ١٥Surah Al-Hashr, ayah 15 (59:15)

كَمَثَلِ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ قَرِيبًا ذَاقُواْ وَبَالَ أَمۡرِهِمۡ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌ ١٥

الصفحة ٥٤٧ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 547 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

They are like their immediate predecessors (the Jews of Banû Qainûqâ‘, who suffered); they tasted the evil result of their conduct, and (in the Hereafter, there is) for them a painful torment.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
