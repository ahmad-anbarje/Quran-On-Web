سورة الحديد، الآية ١٥Surah Al-Hadid, ayah 15 (57:15)

فَٱلۡيَوۡمَ لَا يُؤۡخَذُ مِنكُمۡ فِدۡيَةٌ وَلَا مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مَأۡوَىٰكُمُ ٱلنَّارُ هِىَ مَوۡلَىٰكُمۡ وَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ ١٥

الصفحة ٥٣٩ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 539 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

So this Day no ransom shall be taken from you (hypocrites), nor of those who disbelieved, (in the Oneness of Allâh - Islâmic Monotheism). Your abode is the Fire. That is your maulâ (friend - proper place), and worst indeed is that destination.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
