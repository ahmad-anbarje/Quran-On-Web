سورة الجاثية، الآية ١٩Surah Al-Jathiyah, ayah 19 (45:19)

إِنَّهُمۡ لَن يُغۡنُواْ عَنكَ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ شَيۡـًٔا وَإِنَّ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ بَعۡضُهُمۡ أَوۡلِيَآءُ بَعۡضٍ وَٱللَّهُ وَلِىُّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ ١٩

الصفحة ٥٠٠ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 500 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

Verily, they can avail you nothing against Allâh (if He wants to punish you). Verily, the Zâlimûn (polytheists, wrong-doers) are Auliyâ’ (protectors, helpers) of one another, but Allâh is the Walî (Helper, Protector) of the Muttaqûn (the pious. See V.2:2).

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
