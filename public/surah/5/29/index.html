سورة المائدة، الآية ٢٩Surah Al-Ma'idah, ayah 29 (5:29)

إِنِّىٓ أُرِيدُ أَن تَبُوٓأَ بِإِثۡمِى وَإِثۡمِكَ فَتَكُونَ مِنۡ أَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلنَّارِ وَذَٰلِكَ جَزَٰٓؤُاْ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ ٢٩

الصفحة ١١٢ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 112 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

"Verily, I intend to let you draw my sin on yourself as well as yours, then you will be one of the dwellers of the Fire; and that is the recompense of the Zâlimûn (polytheists and wrong-doers)."

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex

التفسير الميسر
