سورة القصص، الآية ٦٤Surah Al-Qasas, ayah 64 (28:64)

وَقِيلَ ٱدۡعُواْ شُرَكَآءَكُمۡ فَدَعَوۡهُمۡ فَلَمۡ يَسۡتَجِيبُواْ لَهُمۡ وَرَأَوُاْ ٱلۡعَذَابَ لَوۡ أَنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ يَهۡتَدُونَ ٦٤

الصفحة ٣٩٣ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 393 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

And it will be said (to them): "Call upon your (so-called) partners (of Allâh)", and they will call upon them, but they will give no answer to them, and they will see the torment. (They will then wish) if only they had been guided!

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
