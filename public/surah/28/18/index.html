سورة القصص، الآية ١٨Surah Al-Qasas, ayah 18 (28:18)

فَأَصۡبَحَ فِى ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ خَآئِفًا يَتَرَقَّبُ فَإِذَا ٱلَّذِى ٱسۡتَنصَرَهُۥ بِٱلۡأَمۡسِ يَسۡتَصۡرِخُهُۥ قَالَ لَهُۥ مُوسَىٰٓ إِنَّكَ لَغَوِىٌّ مُّبِينٌ ١٨

الصفحة ٣٨٧ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 387 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

So he became afraid, looking about in the city (waiting as to what will be the result of his crime of killing), when behold, the man who had sought his help the day before, called for his help (again). Mûsâ (Moses) said to him: "Verily, you are a plain misleader!"

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
