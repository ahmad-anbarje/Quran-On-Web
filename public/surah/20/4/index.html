سورة طه، الآية ٤Surah Taha, ayah 4 (20:4)

تَنزِيلًا مِّمَّنۡ خَلَقَ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ ٱلۡعُلَى ٤

The meaning in English

A revelation from Him (Allâh) Who has created the earth and high heavens.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
