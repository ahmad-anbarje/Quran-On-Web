سورة طه، الآية ٢٤Surah Taha, ayah 24 (20:24)

ٱذۡهَبۡ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ إِنَّهُۥ طَغَىٰ ٢٤

الصفحة ٣١٣ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 313 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

"Go to Fir‘aun (Pharaoh)! Verily, he has transgressed (all bounds in disbelief and disobedience, and has behaved as an arrogant and as a tyrant)."

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
