سورة طه، الآية ٥٨Surah Taha, ayah 58 (20:58)

فَلَنَأۡتِيَنَّكَ بِسِحۡرٍ مِّثۡلِهِۦ فَٱجۡعَلۡ بَيۡنَنَا وَبَيۡنَكَ مَوۡعِدًا لَّا نُخۡلِفُهُۥ نَحۡنُ وَلَآ أَنتَ مَكَانًا سُوًى ٥٨

الصفحة ٣١٥ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 315 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

"Then verily, we can produce magic the like thereof; so appoint a meeting between us and you, which neither we nor you shall fail to keep, in an open place where both shall have a just and equal chance (and beholders could witness the competition)."

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
