سورة طه، الآية ١٠١Surah Taha, ayah 101 (20:101)

خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهِ وَسَآءَ لَهُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ حِمۡلًا ١٠١

الصفحة ٣١٩ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 319 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

They will abide in that (state in the Fire of Hell) - and evil indeed will it be that load for them on the Day of Resurrection;

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
