سورة طه، الآية ١١٦Surah Taha, ayah 116 (20:116)

وَإِذۡ قُلۡنَا لِلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ ٱسۡجُدُواْ لِـَٔادَمَ فَسَجَدُوٓاْ إِلَّآ إِبۡلِيسَ أَبَىٰ ١١٦

الصفحة ٣٢٠ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 320 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

And (remember) when We said to the angels: "Prostrate yourselves to Adam." They prostrated themselves (all) except Iblîs (Satan) he refused.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
