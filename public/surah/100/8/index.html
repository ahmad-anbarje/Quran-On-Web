سورة العاديات، الآية ٨Surah Al-'Adiyat, ayah 8 (100:8)

وَإِنَّهُۥ لِحُبِّ ٱلۡخَيۡرِ لَشَدِيدٌ ٨

The meaning in English

And verily, he is violent in the love of wealth.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
