سورة التوبة، الآية ٨Surah At-Tawbah, ayah 8 (9:8)

كَيۡفَ وَإِن يَظۡهَرُواْ عَلَيۡكُمۡ لَا يَرۡقُبُواْ فِيكُمۡ إِلًّا وَلَا ذِمَّةً يُرۡضُونَكُم بِأَفۡوَٰهِهِمۡ وَتَأۡبَىٰ قُلُوبُهُمۡ وَأَكۡثَرُهُمۡ فَٰسِقُونَ ٨

الصفحة ١٨٨ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 188 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

How (can there be such a covenant with them) that when you are overpowered by them, they regard not the ties, either of kinship or of covenant with you? With (good words from) their mouths they please you, but their hearts are averse to you, and most of them are Fâsiqûn (rebellious, disobedient to Allâh).

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
