سورة الصافات، الآية ١٥٧Surah As-Saffat, ayah 157 (37:157)

فَأۡتُواْ بِكِتَٰبِكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ ١٥٧

The meaning in English

Then bring your Book if you are truthful!

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
