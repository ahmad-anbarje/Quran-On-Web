سورة الصافات، الآية ١٣٦Surah As-Saffat, ayah 136 (37:136)

ثُمَّ دَمَّرۡنَا ٱلۡـَٔاخَرِينَ ١٣٦

The meaning in English

Then We destroyed the rest (the town of Sodom at the place of the Dead Sea now in Palestine).

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
