سورة الصافات، الآية ٥٤Surah As-Saffat, ayah 54 (37:54)

قَالَ هَلۡ أَنتُم مُّطَّلِعُونَ ٥٤

The meaning in English

(The speaker) said: "Will you look down?"

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex

التفسير الميسر
