سورة الأنفال، الآية ٨Surah Al-Anfal, ayah 8 (8:8)

لِيُحِقَّ ٱلۡحَقَّ وَيُبۡطِلَ ٱلۡبَٰطِلَ وَلَوۡ كَرِهَ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُونَ ٨

الصفحة ١٧٧ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 177 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

That He might cause the truth to triumph and bring falsehood to nothing, even though the Mujrimûn (disbelievers, polytheists, sinners, criminals) hate it.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
