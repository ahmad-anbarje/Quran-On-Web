سورة الأنفال، الآية ٥٢Surah Al-Anfal, ayah 52 (8:52)

كَدَأۡبِ ءَالِ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ كَفَرُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ فَأَخَذَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ بِذُنُوبِهِمۡ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ قَوِىٌّ شَدِيدُ ٱلۡعِقَابِ ٥٢

الصفحة ١٨٣ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 183 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

Similar to the behaviour of the people of Fir‘aun (Pharaoh), and of those before them - they rejected the Ayât (proofs, verses, etc.) of Allâh, so Allâh punished them for their sins. Verily, Allâh is All-Strong, Severe in punishment.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
