سورة ص، الآية ١٩Surah Sad, ayah 19 (38:19)

وَٱلطَّيۡرَ مَحۡشُورَةً كُلٌّ لَّهُۥٓ أَوَّابٌ ١٩

الصفحة ٤٥٤ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 454 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

And (so did) the birds assembled: all obedient to him [Dâwûd (David)] [i.e. they came and glorified Allâh’s Praises along with him]. (Tafsir Al-Qurtubi ).

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
