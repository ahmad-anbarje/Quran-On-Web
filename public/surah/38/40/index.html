سورة ص، الآية ٤٠Surah Sad, ayah 40 (38:40)

وَإِنَّ لَهُۥ عِندَنَا لَزُلۡفَىٰ وَحُسۡنَ مَـَٔابٍ ٤٠

الصفحة ٤٥٥ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 455 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

And verily, for him is a near access to Us, and a good (final) return (Paradise).

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
