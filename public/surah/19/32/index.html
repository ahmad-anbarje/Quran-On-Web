وَبَرًّۢا بِوَٰلِدَتِى وَلَمۡ يَجۡعَلۡنِى جَبَّارًا شَقِيًّا ٣٢

The meaning in English

"And dutiful to my mother, and made me not arrogant, unblest.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
