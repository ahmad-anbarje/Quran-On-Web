سورة مريم، الآية ٦Surah Maryam, ayah 6 (19:6)

يَرِثُنِى وَيَرِثُ مِنۡ ءَالِ يَعۡقُوبَ وَٱجۡعَلۡهُ رَبِّ رَضِيًّا ٦

الصفحة ٣٠٥ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 305 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

"Who shall inherit me, and inherit (also) the posterity of Ya‘qûb (Jacob) (inheritance of the religious knowledge and Prophethood, not of wealth.). And make him, my Lord, one with whom You are Well-Pleased!"

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex

التفسير الميسر
