سورة مريم، الآية ٥٤Surah Maryam, ayah 54 (19:54)

وَٱذۡكُرۡ فِى ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ إِسۡمَٰعِيلَ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ صَادِقَ ٱلۡوَعۡدِ وَكَانَ رَسُولًا نَّبِيًّا ٥٤

الصفحة ٣٠٩ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 309 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

And mention in the Book (the Qur’ân) Ismâ‘îl (Ishmael). Verily he was true to what he promised, and he was a Messenger, (and) a Prophet.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
