سورة مريم، الآية ١٠Surah Maryam, ayah 10 (19:10)

قَالَ رَبِّ ٱجۡعَل لِّىٓ ءَايَةً قَالَ ءَايَتُكَ أَلَّا تُكَلِّمَ ٱلنَّاسَ ثَلَٰثَ لَيَالٍ سَوِيًّا ١٠

الصفحة ٣٠٥ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 305 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

[Zakariyyâ (Zachariah)] said: "My Lord! Appoint for me a sign." He said: "Your sign is that you shall not speak unto mankind for three nights, though having no bodily defect."

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
