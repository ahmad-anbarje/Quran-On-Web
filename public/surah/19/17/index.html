سورة مريم، الآية ١٧Surah Maryam, ayah 17 (19:17)

فَٱتَّخَذَتۡ مِن دُونِهِمۡ حِجَابًا فَأَرۡسَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡهَا رُوحَنَا فَتَمَثَّلَ لَهَا بَشَرًا سَوِيًّا ١٧

الصفحة ٣٠٦ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 306 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

She placed a screen (to screen herself) from them; then We sent to her Our Ruh [angel Jibrîl (Gabriel)], and he appeared before her in the form of a man in all respects.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
