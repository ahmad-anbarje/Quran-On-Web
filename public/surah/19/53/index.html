سورة مريم، الآية ٥٣Surah Maryam, ayah 53 (19:53)

وَوَهَبۡنَا لَهُۥ مِن رَّحۡمَتِنَآ أَخَاهُ هَٰرُونَ نَبِيًّا ٥٣

الصفحة ٣٠٩ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 309 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

And We granted him his brother Hârûn (Aaron), (also) a Prophet, out of Our Mercy.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
