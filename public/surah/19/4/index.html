سورة مريم، الآية ٤Surah Maryam, ayah 4 (19:4)

قَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّى وَهَنَ ٱلۡعَظۡمُ مِنِّى وَٱشۡتَعَلَ ٱلرَّأۡسُ شَيۡبًا وَلَمۡ أَكُنۢ بِدُعَآئِكَ رَبِّ شَقِيًّا ٤

الصفحة ٣٠٥ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 305 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

He said: "My Lord! Indeed my bones have grown feeble, and grey hair has spread on my head, And I have never been unblest in my invocation to You, O my Lord!

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
