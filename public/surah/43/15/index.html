سورة الزخرف، الآية ١٥Surah Az-Zukhruf, ayah 15 (43:15)

وَجَعَلُواْ لَهُۥ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦ جُزۡءًا إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَكَفُورٌ مُّبِينٌ ١٥

الصفحة ٤٩٠ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 490 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

Yet, they assign to some of His slaves a share with Him (by pretending that He has children, and considering them as equals or co-partners in worship with Him). Verily, man is indeed a manifest ingrate!

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex

التفسير الميسر
