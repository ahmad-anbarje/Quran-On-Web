سورة الشعراء، الآية ٧١Surah Ash-Shu'ara, ayah 71 (26:71)

قَالُواْ نَعۡبُدُ أَصۡنَامًا فَنَظَلُّ لَهَا عَٰكِفِينَ ٧١

The meaning in English

They said: "We worship idols, and to them we are ever devoted."

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
