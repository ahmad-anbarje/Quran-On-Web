سورة الشعراء، الآية ٤٠Surah Ash-Shu'ara, ayah 40 (26:40)

لَعَلَّنَا نَتَّبِعُ ٱلسَّحَرَةَ إِن كَانُواْ هُمُ ٱلۡغَٰلِبِينَ ٤٠

الصفحة ٣٦٩ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 369 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

"That we may follow the sorcerers [who were on Fir‘aun’s (Pharaoh) religion of disbelief] if they are the winners."

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
