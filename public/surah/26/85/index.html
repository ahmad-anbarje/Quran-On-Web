سورة الشعراء، الآية ٨٥Surah Ash-Shu'ara, ayah 85 (26:85)

وَٱجۡعَلۡنِى مِن وَرَثَةِ جَنَّةِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ ٨٥

The meaning in English

And make me one of the inheritors of the Paradise of Delight.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
