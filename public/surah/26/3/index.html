سورة الشعراء، الآية ٣Surah Ash-Shu'ara, ayah 3 (26:3)

لَعَلَّكَ بَٰخِعٌ نَّفۡسَكَ أَلَّا يَكُونُواْ مُؤۡمِنِينَ ٣

الصفحة ٣٦٧ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 367 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

It may be that you (O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم) are going to kill yourself with grief, that they do not become believers [in your Risalah (Messengership) i.e. in your Message of Islâmic Monotheism].

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
