سورة القدر، الآية ٥Surah Al-Qadr, ayah 5 (97:5)

سَلَٰمٌ هِىَ حَتَّىٰ مَطۡلَعِ ٱلۡفَجۡرِ ٥

الصفحة ٥٩٨ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 598 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

(All that night), there is Peace (and Goodness from Allâh to His believing slaves) until the appearance of dawn.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex

التفسير الميسر
