سورة الطور، الآية ١٨Surah At-Tur, ayah 18 (52:18)

فَٰكِهِينَ بِمَآ ءَاتَىٰهُمۡ رَبُّهُمۡ وَوَقَىٰهُمۡ رَبُّهُمۡ عَذَابَ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ ١٨

الصفحة ٥٢٤ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 524 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

Enjoying in that which their Lord has bestowed on them, and (the fact that) their Lord saved them from the torment of the blazing Fire.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
