سورة النمل، الآية ٨٧Surah An-Naml, ayah 87 (27:87)

وَيَوۡمَ يُنفَخُ فِى ٱلصُّورِ فَفَزِعَ مَن فِى ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَن فِى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ إِلَّا مَن شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ وَكُلٌّ أَتَوۡهُ دَٰخِرِينَ ٨٧

الصفحة ٣٨٤ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 384 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

And (remember) the Day on which the Trumpet will be blown - and all who are in the heavens and all who are on the earth, will be terrified except him whom Allâh will (exempt). And all shall come to Him, humbled.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
