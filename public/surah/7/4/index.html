سورة الأعراف، الآية ٤Surah Al-A'raf, ayah 4 (7:4)

وَكَم مِّن قَرۡيَةٍ أَهۡلَكۡنَٰهَا فَجَآءَهَا بَأۡسُنَا بَيَٰتًا أَوۡ هُمۡ قَآئِلُونَ ٤

الصفحة ١٥١ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 151 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

And a great number of towns (their population) We destroyed (for their crimes). Our torment came upon them (suddenly) by night or while they were taking their midday nap.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
