سورة الأعراف، الآية ٩٥Surah Al-A'raf, ayah 95 (7:95)

ثُمَّ بَدَّلۡنَا مَكَانَ ٱلسَّيِّئَةِ ٱلۡحَسَنَةَ حَتَّىٰ عَفَواْ وَّقَالُواْ قَدۡ مَسَّ ءَابَآءَنَا ٱلضَّرَّآءُ وَٱلسَّرَّآءُ فَأَخَذۡنَٰهُم بَغۡتَةً وَهُمۡ لَا يَشۡعُرُونَ ٩٥

الصفحة ١٦٢ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 162 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

Then We changed the evil for the good, until they increased in number and in wealth, and said: "Our fathers were touched with evil (loss of health and calamities) and with good (prosperity)." So We seized them all of a sudden while they were unaware.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
