سورة الأعراف، الآية ١٤٨Surah Al-A'raf, ayah 148 (7:148)

وَٱتَّخَذَ قَوۡمُ مُوسَىٰ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦ مِنۡ حُلِيِّهِمۡ عِجۡلًا جَسَدًا لَّهُۥ خُوَارٌ أَلَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ أَنَّهُۥ لَا يُكَلِّمُهُمۡ وَلَا يَهۡدِيهِمۡ سَبِيلًا ٱتَّخَذُوهُ وَكَانُواْ ظَٰلِمِينَ ١٤٨

الصفحة ١٦٨ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 168 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

And the people of Mûsâ (Moses) made in his absence, out of their ornaments, the image of a calf (for worship). It had a sound (as if it was mooing). Did they not see that it could neither speak to them nor guide them to the way? They took it (for worship) and they were Zâlimûn (wrong-doers).

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
