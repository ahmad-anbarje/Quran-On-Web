سورة الأعراف، الآية ٦٤Surah Al-A'raf, ayah 64 (7:64)

فَكَذَّبُوهُ فَأَنجَيۡنَٰهُ وَٱلَّذِينَ مَعَهُۥ فِى ٱلۡفُلۡكِ وَأَغۡرَقۡنَا ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَآ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَوۡمًا عَمِينَ ٦٤

الصفحة ١٥٨ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 158 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

But they belied him, so We saved him and those along with him in the ship, and We drowned those who belied Our Ayât (proofs, evidence, verses, lessons, signs, revelations, etc.). They were indeed a blind people.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
