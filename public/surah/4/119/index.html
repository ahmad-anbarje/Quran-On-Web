سورة النساء، الآية ١١٩Surah An-Nisa, ayah 119 (4:119)

وَلَأُضِلَّنَّهُمۡ وَلَأُمَنِّيَنَّهُمۡ وَلَـَٔامُرَنَّهُمۡ فَلَيُبَتِّكُنَّ ءَاذَانَ ٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ وَلَـَٔامُرَنَّهُمۡ فَلَيُغَيِّرُنَّ خَلۡقَ ٱللَّهِ وَمَن يَتَّخِذِ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَ وَلِيًّا مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ فَقَدۡ خَسِرَ خُسۡرَانًا مُّبِينًا ١١٩

The meaning in English

Verily, I will mislead them, and surely, I will arouse in them false desires; and certainly, I will order them to slit the ears of cattle, and indeed I will order them to change the nature created by Allâh." And whoever takes Shaitân (Satan) as a Walî (protector or helper) instead of Allâh, has surely suffered a manifest loss.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
