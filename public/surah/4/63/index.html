سورة النساء، الآية ٦٣Surah An-Nisa, ayah 63 (4:63)

أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَعۡلَمُ ٱللَّهُ مَا فِى قُلُوبِهِمۡ فَأَعۡرِضۡ عَنۡهُمۡ وَعِظۡهُمۡ وَقُل لَّهُمۡ فِىٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ قَوۡلًۢا بَلِيغًا ٦٣

الصفحة ٨٨ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 88 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

They (hypocrites) are those of whom Allâh knows what is in their hearts; so turn aside from them (do not punish them) but admonish them, and speak to them an effective word (i.e. to believe in Allâh, worship Him, obey Him, and be afraid of Him) to reach their innerselves.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
