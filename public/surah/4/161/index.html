سورة النساء، الآية ١٦١Surah An-Nisa, ayah 161 (4:161)

وَأَخۡذِهِمُ ٱلرِّبَوٰاْ وَقَدۡ نُهُواْ عَنۡهُ وَأَكۡلِهِمۡ أَمۡوَٰلَ ٱلنَّاسِ بِٱلۡبَٰطِلِ وَأَعۡتَدۡنَا لِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ مِنۡهُمۡ عَذَابًا أَلِيمًا ١٦١

الصفحة ١٠٣ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 103 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

And their taking of Ribâ (usury) though they were forbidden from taking it and their devouring of men’s substance wrongfully (bribery). And We have prepared for the disbelievers among them a painful torment.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
