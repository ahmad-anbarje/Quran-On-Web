سورة الشرح، الآية ٥Surah Ash-Sharh, ayah 5 (94:5)

فَإِنَّ مَعَ ٱلۡعُسۡرِ يُسۡرًا ٥

The meaning in English

Verily, along with every hardship is relief,

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
