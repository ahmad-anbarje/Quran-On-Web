سورة الكهف، الآية ٥٨Surah Al-Kahf, ayah 58 (18:58)

وَرَبُّكَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ذُو ٱلرَّحۡمَةِ لَوۡ يُؤَاخِذُهُم بِمَا كَسَبُواْ لَعَجَّلَ لَهُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابَ بَل لَّهُم مَّوۡعِدٌ لَّن يَجِدُواْ مِن دُونِهِۦ مَوۡئِلًا ٥٨

The meaning in English

And your Lord is Most Forgiving, Owner of Mercy. Were He to call them to account for what they have earned, then surely, He would have hastened their punishment. But they have their appointed time, beyond which they will find no escape.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex

التفسير الميسر
