سورة محمد، الآية ٩Surah Muhammad, ayah 9 (47:9)

ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ كَرِهُواْ مَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ فَأَحۡبَطَ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ ٩

الصفحة ٥٠٧ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 507 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

That is because they hate that which Allâh has sent down (this Qur’ân and Islâmic laws, etc.); so He has made their deeds fruitless.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex

التفسير الميسر
