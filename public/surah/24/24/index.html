سورة النور، الآية ٢٤Surah An-Nur, ayah 24 (24:24)

يَوۡمَ تَشۡهَدُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ أَلۡسِنَتُهُمۡ وَأَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَأَرۡجُلُهُم بِمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ ٢٤

الصفحة ٣٥٢ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 352 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

On the Day when their tongues, their hands, and their legs (or feet) will bear witness against them as to what they used to do.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
