سورة السجدة، الآية ٢١Surah As-Sajdah, ayah 21 (32:21)

وَلَنُذِيقَنَّهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡعَذَابِ ٱلۡأَدۡنَىٰ دُونَ ٱلۡعَذَابِ ٱلۡأَكۡبَرِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ ٢١

الصفحة ٤١٧ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 417 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

And verily, We will make them taste of the near torment (i.e. the torment in the life of this world, i.e. disasters, calamities, etc.) prior to the supreme torment (in the Hereafter), in order that they may (repent and) return (i.e. accept Islâm).

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex

التفسير الميسر
