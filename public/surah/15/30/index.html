سورة الحجر، الآية ٣٠Surah Al-Hijr, ayah 30 (15:30)

فَسَجَدَ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ كُلُّهُمۡ أَجۡمَعُونَ ٣٠

The meaning in English

So the angels prostrated themselves, all of them together.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex

التفسير الميسر
