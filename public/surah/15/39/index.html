سورة الحجر، الآية ٣٩Surah Al-Hijr, ayah 39 (15:39)

قَالَ رَبِّ بِمَآ أَغۡوَيۡتَنِى لَأُزَيِّنَنَّ لَهُمۡ فِى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَأُغۡوِيَنَّهُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ ٣٩

الصفحة ٢٦٤ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 264 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

[Iblîs (Satan)] said: "O my Lord! Because you misled me, I shall indeed adorn the path of error for them (mankind) on the earth, and I shall mislead them all.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
