سورة الجن، الآية ١٧Surah Al-Jinn, ayah 17 (72:17)

لِّنَفۡتِنَهُمۡ فِيهِ وَمَن يُعۡرِضۡ عَن ذِكۡرِ رَبِّهِۦ يَسۡلُكۡهُ عَذَابًا صَعَدًا ١٧

الصفحة ٥٧٣ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 573 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

That We might try them thereby. And whosoever turns away from the Reminder of his Lord (i.e. this Qur’ân, - and practise not its laws and orders), He will cause him to enter in a severe torment (i.e. Hell).

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
