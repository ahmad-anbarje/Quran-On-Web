سورة الحج، الآية ٥٣Surah Al-Hajj, ayah 53 (22:53)

لِّيَجۡعَلَ مَا يُلۡقِى ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ فِتۡنَةً لِّلَّذِينَ فِى قُلُوبِهِم مَّرَضٌ وَٱلۡقَاسِيَةِ قُلُوبُهُمۡ وَإِنَّ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ لَفِى شِقَاقٍۭ بَعِيدٍ ٥٣

الصفحة ٣٣٨ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 338 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

That He (Allâh) may make what is thrown in by Shaitân (Satan) a trial for those in whose hearts is a disease (of hypocrisy and disbelief) and whose hearts are hardened. And certainly, the Zalimûn (polytheists and wrong-doers) are in an opposition far-off (from the truth against Allâh’s Messenger and the believers).

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
