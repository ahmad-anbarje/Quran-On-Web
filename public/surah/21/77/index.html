سورة الأنبياء، الآية ٧٧Surah Al-Anbya, ayah 77 (21:77)

وَنَصَرۡنَٰهُ مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَآ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَوۡمَ سَوۡءٍ فَأَغۡرَقۡنَٰهُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ ٧٧

الصفحة ٣٢٨ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 328 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

We helped him against the people who denied Our Ayât (proofs, evidence, verses, lessons, signs, revelations, etc.). Verily, they were a people given to evil. So We drowned them all.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
