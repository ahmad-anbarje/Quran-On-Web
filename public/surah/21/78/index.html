سورة الأنبياء، الآية ٧٨Surah Al-Anbya, ayah 78 (21:78)

وَدَاوُۥدَ وَسُلَيۡمَٰنَ إِذۡ يَحۡكُمَانِ فِى ٱلۡحَرۡثِ إِذۡ نَفَشَتۡ فِيهِ غَنَمُ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ وَكُنَّا لِحُكۡمِهِمۡ شَٰهِدِينَ ٧٨

الصفحة ٣٢٨ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 328 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

And (remember) Dâwûd (David) and Sulaimân (Solomon), when they gave judgement in the case of the field in which the sheep of certain people had pastured at night; and We were witness to their judgement.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
