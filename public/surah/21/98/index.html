سورة الأنبياء، الآية ٩٨Surah Al-Anbya, ayah 98 (21:98)

إِنَّكُمۡ وَمَا تَعۡبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ حَصَبُ جَهَنَّمَ أَنتُمۡ لَهَا وَٰرِدُونَ ٩٨

الصفحة ٣٣٠ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 330 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

Certainly you (disbelievers) and that which you are worshipping now besides Allâh, are (but) fuel for Hell! (Surely), you will enter it.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
