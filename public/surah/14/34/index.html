سورة ابراهيم، الآية ٣٤Surah Ibrahim, ayah 34 (14:34)

وَءَاتَىٰكُم مِّن كُلِّ مَا سَأَلۡتُمُوهُ وَإِن تَعُدُّواْ نِعۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ لَا تُحۡصُوهَآ إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَظَلُومٌ كَفَّارٌ ٣٤

الصفحة ٢٦٠ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 260 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

And He gave you of all that you asked for, and if you count the Blessings of Allâh, never will you be able to count them. Verily, man is indeed an extreme wrong-doer, a disbeliever (an extreme ingrate who denies Allâh’s Blessings by disbelief, and by worshipping others besides Allâh, and by disobeying Allâh and His Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه و سلم).

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
