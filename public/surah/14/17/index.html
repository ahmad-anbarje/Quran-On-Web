سورة ابراهيم، الآية ١٧Surah Ibrahim, ayah 17 (14:17)

يَتَجَرَّعُهُۥ وَلَا يَكَادُ يُسِيغُهُۥ وَيَأۡتِيهِ ٱلۡمَوۡتُ مِن كُلِّ مَكَانٍ وَمَا هُوَ بِمَيِّتٍ وَمِن وَرَآئِهِۦ عَذَابٌ غَلِيظٌ ١٧

الصفحة ٢٥٧ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 257 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

He will sip it unwillingly, and he will find a great difficulty to swallow it down his throat; and death will come to him from every side, yet he will not die and in front of him, will be a great torment.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
