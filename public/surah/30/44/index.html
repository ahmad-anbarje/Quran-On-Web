سورة الروم، الآية ٤٤Surah Ar-Rum, ayah 44 (30:44)

مَن كَفَرَ فَعَلَيۡهِ كُفۡرُهُۥ وَمَنۡ عَمِلَ صَٰلِحًا فَلِأَنفُسِهِمۡ يَمۡهَدُونَ ٤٤

الصفحة ٤٠٩ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 409 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

Whosoever disbelieves will suffer from his disbelief, and whosoever does righteous good deeds (by practising Islâmic Monotheism), then such will prepare a good place (in Paradise) for themselves (and will be saved by Allâh from His Torment).

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex

التفسير الميسر
