سورة لقمان، الآية ٦Surah Luqman, ayah 6 (31:6)

وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَن يَشۡتَرِى لَهۡوَ ٱلۡحَدِيثِ لِيُضِلَّ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ بِغَيۡرِ عِلۡمٍ وَيَتَّخِذَهَا هُزُوًا أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ مُّهِينٌ ٦

الصفحة ٤١١ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 411 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

And of mankind is he who purchases idle talks (i.e. music, singing, etc.) to mislead (men) from the Path of Allâh without knowledge, and takes it (the Path of Allâh, or the Verses of the Qur’ân) by way of mockery. For such there will be a humiliating torment (in the Hell-fire).

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex

التفسير الميسر
