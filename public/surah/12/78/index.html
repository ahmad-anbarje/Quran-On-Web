سورة يوسف، الآية ٧٨Surah Yusuf, ayah 78 (12:78)

قَالُواْ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡعَزِيزُ إِنَّ لَهُۥٓ أَبًا شَيۡخًا كَبِيرًا فَخُذۡ أَحَدَنَا مَكَانَهُۥٓ إِنَّا نَرَىٰكَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ ٧٨

الصفحة ٢٤٤ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 244 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

They said: "O ruler of the land! Verily, he has an old father (who will grieve for him); so take one of us in his place. Indeed we think that you are one of the Muhsinûn (good-doers - see the footnote of V.9:120)."

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
