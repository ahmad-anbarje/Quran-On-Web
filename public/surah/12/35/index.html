سورة يوسف، الآية ٣٥Surah Yusuf, ayah 35 (12:35)

ثُمَّ بَدَا لَهُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا رَأَوُاْ ٱلۡـَٔايَٰتِ لَيَسۡجُنُنَّهُۥ حَتَّىٰ حِينٍ ٣٥

الصفحة ٢٣٩ من مصحف المدينة · اقرأها في المصحف مع التلاوة
Page 239 of the Madinah Mushaf · Read it in the mushaf, with recitation

The meaning in English

Then it occurred to them, after they had seen the proofs (of his innocence), to imprison him for a time.

Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali and Muhammad Muhsin Khan, King Fahd Complex
