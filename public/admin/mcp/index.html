How callers are told apart. Claude.ai and ChatGPT are verified by the address ranges Anthropic and OpenAI publish for their servers' calls; their many readers arrive from a few addresses, so they show as a few users. Apps on a reader's own machine (Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code, Gemini CLI, Codex) are known by the name they give; scanners by theirs; anything else is unverified. Users are told apart by a code made from their address with a key that changes every day and is never saved, so they are counted per day and added up over a period. No address, no list of people and nothing anyone typed is kept; the recent calls hold only the choices, in memory, and go when the server restarts.
