"Users through AI assistants" counts the callers whose app is a known assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, VS Code, Cursor…); the rest are scanners. By country covers both. Users are callers told apart by a code made from their address with a key that changes every day and is never saved, so they are counted per day and added up over a period; no address, no list of people and nothing asked is kept. Apps that run in the cloud (ChatGPT, claude.ai) call from their own servers, so their many readers show as a few users, in the country of those servers; apps on a reader's own computer (Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code) show the reader's country. A connection is an assistant opening the server, about once a conversation.
