How this is counted. Claude.ai and ChatGPT are recognised by the address ranges Anthropic and OpenAI publish; all their readers arrive through those servers, so how many people use them cannot be counted, only their questions. Apps on a person's own machine (Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code, Gemini CLI, Codex) are known by the name they give, and each address is one person a day. Scanners are known by their names. Calls from wherever this page was last opened today are your own testing and are kept out of every reader count. No address, no list of people and nothing anyone typed is kept; the recent calls hold only the choices, in memory, and go when the server restarts.
